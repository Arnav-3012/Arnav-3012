## About

I'm a Data Science student at NMIMS MPSTME, Mumbai (BTech, CGPA 3.83/4, graduating 2028), building production AI systems: RAG, agents and LLM tooling. I like problems where I have to learn something new to finish them, and I'd rather ship something real than polish a slide.

- **Curious by default.** I learn by taking things apart: training small LLMs from scratch, reading papers, re-deriving the maths until it clicks.
- **Builder first.** If an idea is worth having, it's worth a working prototype.
- **Measure everything.** Cost, latency, accuracy, calibration. If I can't measure it, I don't trust it.
- **Honest feedback over comfort.** I want the critical review. It's how I improve fastest.
- **Founder mindset.** Running a D2C brand taught me to own a problem end to end, not just my slice of it.
- **Always a student.** New stack, new domain, new paper. I'd rather be the least experienced person in the room.
