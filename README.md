## 🧠 ABOUT ME

I'm Arnav, a Data Science student in Mumbai who builds AI systems and runs a clothing brand on the side. I like projects where I have to learn something new to finish them, and I'd rather ship something real than polish a slide.

**How I work**

- 🔍 **Curious by default.** I learn by taking things apart: training small LLMs from scratch, reading papers, re-deriving the maths until it clicks.
- 🛠️ **Builder first.** If an idea is worth having, it's worth a working prototype. Most of my projects started as "can I actually build this?"
- 📏 **Measure everything.** Cost, latency, accuracy, calibration. If I can't measure it, I don't trust it.
- 🪞 **Honest feedback over comfort.** I want the critical review, not the compliment. It's how I improve fastest.
- 🚀 **Founder mindset.** Running BEINN ("Be Real") taught me to own a problem end to end, not just my slice of it.
- 🌱 **Always a student.** New stack, new domain, new paper: I'd rather be the least experienced person in the room than the most.
